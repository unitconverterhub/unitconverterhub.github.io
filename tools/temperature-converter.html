Temperature Converter

Convert temperature between Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin. Water freezes at 0 °C (32 °F) and boils at 100 °C (212 °F).

How it works: Temperature is converted through Celsius: Fahrenheit to Celsius is (F − 32) × 5/9, and Kelvin is Celsius + 273.15. All calculations run in your browser with JavaScript — no data is sent anywhere and no internet connection is needed after the page loads.
