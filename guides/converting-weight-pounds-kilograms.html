Pounds to Kilograms and Back: A Complete Weight Conversion Guide

UnitConverter guides · Measurement

Weight is one of the first measurements we learn and one of the most frequently converted. Recipes switch between grams and ounces, luggage limits are quoted in kilograms in one country and pounds in another, and fitness goals are tracked in both systems depending on where you live. A kilogram is about 2.2 pounds, which is close enough for a rough guess but not close enough for a pharmacy, a laboratory or an airline check-in desk. This guide gives you the exact factors and the practical tricks to move between systems with confidence.

The exact factors you need

The foundation of all weight conversion is the exact definition of the pound: one avoirdupois pound equals exactly 453.59237 grams. That single definition fixes every other imperial weight unit, because an ounce is one sixteenth of a pound (28.349523125 grams) and a stone is fourteen pounds (6.35029318 kilograms). On the metric side, the relationships are decimal: one thousand grams make a kilogram, one thousand kilograms make a tonne, and one milligram is a thousandth of a gram.

To convert pounds to kilograms, multiply by 0.45359237; to convert kilograms to pounds, multiply by 2.20462262. To convert ounces to grams, multiply by 28.349523125. These factors are exact by definition, so any converter that uses them will give a perfectly accurate answer. The familiar shortcut of 2.2 pounds per kilogram introduces an error of about 0.2 percent — negligible for a bag of flour, but enough to matter when you are weighing out medication or calibrating a scale.

Weight in the kitchen

Cooking is where most people meet weight conversion, and it is also where accuracy matters most. Baking in particular is chemistry: the ratio of flour to water to yeast determines whether bread rises or collapses, and a ten percent error in flour can ruin a loaf. Professional bakers weigh ingredients in grams because grams are small enough to be precise and large enough to be practical. A cup of flour can weigh anywhere from 120 to 150 grams depending on how it is scooped, which is why recipes that give weights are more reliable than recipes that give volumes.

When converting a recipe, convert every ingredient by weight rather than guessing cup equivalents. Butter is the easiest: one stick of American butter is 113 grams, almost exactly half a cup. Sugar is about 200 grams per cup, flour about 120 grams per cup, and honey about 340 grams per cup. Keep a kitchen scale on the bench and weigh in grams — it is faster than washing measuring cups, more accurate, and it makes any recipe from any country immediately usable.

Weight for travel and luggage

Airline luggage limits are the most expensive place to get a weight conversion wrong. A typical checked-bag limit is 23 kilograms, which is 50.7 pounds, and most airlines enforce the limit strictly at the check-in desk. If you pack to 50 pounds thinking the limit is 23 kilograms, you are already over by more than half a kilogram. Conversely, a 20-kilogram limit is 44 pounds, and a 32-kilogram limit for premium cabins is 70.5 pounds. Weigh your bag at home on a bathroom scale — weigh yourself, then weigh yourself holding the bag, and subtract.

Hand luggage is usually limited to 7 or 10 kilograms, which is 15.4 or 22 pounds. That sounds generous until you add a laptop, a camera and a thick book. A practical approach is to pack in kilograms when the airline quotes kilograms, and to keep a small luggage scale in your bag for the return journey, when souvenirs have a way of adding weight. Remember that the limit applies to the total weight including the bag itself, and that a heavy suitcase can eat two or three kilograms of your allowance before you pack a single shirt.

Weight in health and fitness

Body weight is the measurement people care about most personally, and it is also the one where the two systems collide most confusingly. In the United States weight is quoted in pounds, in most of Europe in kilograms, and in Britain in stones and pounds — a person might be 12 stone 6 pounds, which is 79 kilograms or 174 pounds. Body mass index, the standard health measure, is calculated in kilograms and metres, so anyone tracking BMI needs to convert their weight into kilograms first.

For fitness tracking, consistency matters more than the unit: weigh yourself at the same time of day, on the same scale, in the same clothes, and record the trend rather than any single reading. A person's weight can vary by one or two kilograms over a day as they eat, drink and exercise. When setting a goal, convert it into the unit your scale uses and stick to that unit — switching between pounds and kilograms mid-program only invites confusion. And remember that muscle is denser than fat, so the scale alone never tells the whole story of your health.
